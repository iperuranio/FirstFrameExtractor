First Frame Extractor — how to open it if macOS blocks the app
==============================================================

macOS blocks unsigned apps downloaded from the internet, with a message like
"Apple could not verify ... is free of malware that may harm your Mac".
This is expected: the app simply isn't code-signed. Here's how to open it.


FIX IT ONCE (Terminal — the reliable way)
-----------------------------------------

1. Drag "First Frame Extractor" into your Applications folder.

2. Open the Terminal app:
   press Cmd-Space, type  Terminal , press Enter.

3. Copy the line below, paste it into Terminal, press Enter:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/First Frame Extractor.app"

4. Open the app normally with a double-click. Done — no more warnings.


ALTERNATIVE (no Terminal)
-------------------------

1. Double-click the app (it will be blocked the first time).
2. Open  System Settings > Privacy & Security .
3. Scroll to the bottom and click  "Open Anyway"  next to
   "First Frame Extractor", then confirm.


Why this happens: removing all warnings entirely would require Apple
notarization (a paid Apple Developer account). The steps above are the free,
standard way to run unsigned apps.
